sql-server.sql is an unmodified upstream snapshot. DO NOT RUN it: its default is destructive.
0002_quartz.up.sql copies only the creation section, twice, replacing QRTZ_ with
WDS_QRTZ_ and WDE_QRTZ_ in table, index and constraint names. It removes upstream
USE and drop-existing logic, adds bootstrap prerequisite and per-table runtime
DML grants. Columns, constraints, indexes and optional history tables are preserved.
0002_quartz.down.sql is WorkerDock code: it refuses populated/active stores and drops
both sets in foreign-key order. The migrator owns transactions and checksums.
