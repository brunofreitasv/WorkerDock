## What you will do

Find the main tenant screens, choose a language, and learn which actions your role allows.

## Before you start

Sign in to your WorkerDock tenant. Available pages depend on whether your account has Read or Manage permission.

## Access roles

Anyone with Read access can browse the dashboard and help guides and open Personal settings. Managing jobs and tenant configuration requires Manage access.

## Steps

1. Open the [dashboard](#/dashboard) to review current activity and recent executions.
2. Use the Monitoring, Maintenance, and Administration groups to move between pages allowed for your role.
3. Open [Settings](#/settings) to choose **Português (Brasil)** or **English** and your personal time zone. The saved tenant display name appears beside the logo and in the browser tab.
4. Managers can open [jobs](#/jobs) to create and manage work. Readers can review job and execution information where available.

## Example

With Read access, review recent executions on the dashboard and use Monitoring to find a job. With Manage access, continue to the [first job guide](#/help:first-job) and configure a synthetic example before enabling it.

## Check the result

You can find the tenant area you need and understand which actions your role permits. A guide explains the current workflow; it does not grant additional access.

## If it does not work

If a screen says access is required, ask a tenant administrator to assign the needed role. If monitoring data is unavailable, check its update time and configured data source before treating an empty view as healthy.
